Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Information Orbit processes

When you connect a Roblox account using OAuth, Orbit receives the account's Roblox user ID, username, display name, profile link, and OAuth tokens. Orbit requests only the openid and profile scopes.

Orbit also stores your launcher settings, saved library, and history of experiences launched through Orbit on your device. Public experience information, including titles, thumbnails, descriptions, ratings, badges, and server availability, is requested from Roblox services.

How information is stored

OAuth tokens and launcher preferences are stored locally on your device. Orbit uses the operating system's protected storage for OAuth tokens when it is available. Orbit does not send OAuth tokens to an Orbit server.

Optional enhanced Roblox session

Orbit offers an optional isolated Roblox browser session for features Roblox does not provide through Orbit's OAuth scopes, such as friend presence. Session cookies remain inside a dedicated browser partition on your device. Orbit does not display, export, or upload those cookies. Choosing “Disconnect and erase session” removes that partition's cookies, storage, and cache.

Third-party services

Orbit communicates with Roblox to authenticate accounts, retrieve Roblox content, and launch experiences. Public discovery requests may pass through Orbit's Cloudflare-hosted API. GitHub Pages hosts this informational website. Those providers process requests under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke OAuth access by signing out of Orbit or through Roblox account settings. You can erase the optional enhanced session from Orbit Settings. Uninstalling Orbit and deleting its application data removes locally saved launcher information.

Children

Roblox requires users to be at least 13 years old to authorize OAuth applications. Orbit does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Orbit changes. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, bug reports, or deletion help, open an issue in the Orbit Launcher GitHub repository.
