Your games.
Your launcher.

Orbit is a customizable Windows launcher for discovering, organizing, and opening Roblox experiences with live game information and friend activity.

View the projectHow Orbit handles data

Discover

Search Roblox experiences and view current thumbnails, descriptions, ratings, badges, and public servers.

Connect

Sign in through Roblox OAuth. Orbit never asks you to paste your Roblox security cookie.

Launch

Open experiences in Roblox Player, join available friends, and keep your own local library and recent history.
